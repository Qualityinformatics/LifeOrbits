Life Orbits — a priority instrument

You can't max out everything.

This version enforces that on purpose. You'll spread a fixed 100 points across Self, Family, and Friends — then spread 100 more inside each circle. Raise one, and points come out of the others in real time, because that's the actual constraint you live under. Time & energy questions are anchored to what "full effort" concretely looks like for that specific thing, since maxing out prayer isn't the same unit as maxing out a study schedule.
